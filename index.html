PhD student, Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Utah

I'm a PhD student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering department at the University of Utah, advised by Dr. Morteza Fayazi. I work on analog electronic design automation, a field that intersects computer engineering and artificial intelligence. Lately I've been focused on using large language models for analog circuit sizing and reinforcement learning for antenna pattern synthesis.

Before Utah, I did my undergraduate degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bangalore.
